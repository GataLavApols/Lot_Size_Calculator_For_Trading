About

Last updated: October 2026

The Lot Size Calculator for Trading is a free, minimal position size calculator for forex pairs, gold (XAUUSD), silver (XAGUSD) and US indices (US30, NAS100, SP500).

What it does

  • Takes your account size, risk (percent or dollars) and stop loss, and returns a lot size instantly.
  • Assumes a USD-denominated account: forex standard lot = 100,000 units, indices 1.0 lot = contract size 1 ($1 per point).
  • Runs 100% in your browser — no account, no tracking, no data leaves your device.

Why it exists

Position sizing is the core of risk management. This tool removes the mental math so traders can risk a fixed, pre-planned amount per trade instead of guessing lot sizes.

Important

This is an educational tool only and is not financial advice. Always confirm contract specifications with your own broker — see the Risk Disclaimer.
