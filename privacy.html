Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. No data collection by this tool

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Account sizes, risk values and prices you enter are never sent to us, stored on a server, or shared. There is no login, no database, and no analytics built into the tool itself.

2. Cookies and advertising (when enabled)

If advertising is enabled in the future (e.g. Google AdSense), Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized advertising where you have consented. You can manage ad personalization at Google's Ad Settings page. A consent banner will be shown where required by law (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK).

3. Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not combine these logs with any personal profile.

4. Third-party links

Links to external sites (e.g. GitHub) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

5. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.
